# LALA Fashion - AI / LLM Context Documentation

## 1. About the Store
**Name:** LALA Fashion
**URL:** https://www.lalafashion.store
**Description:** Modern Lifestyle store based in Pakistan offering premium watches, stylish glasses, exquisite jewellery, and the latest electronics. Fast delivery across Pakistan.

## 2. Site Structure & Important Pages
The application is a Next.js (App Router) commerce platform.

### Core E-commerce Pages
- **Home:** `/`
- **Shop (All Products):** `/shop`
- **Product Details:** `/product/[slug]` (e.g., `/product/smart-watch-series-9`)
- **Categories:** `/category/[slug]`
- **Cart/Checkout:** `/checkout` (Handled dynamically)
- **Order Success:** `/success`
- **Track Order:** `/track-order`

### Customer Portal
- **Dashboard:** `/customer`
- **Login/Register:** `/customer/login`, `/customer/register`
- **Profile / Orders:** `/customer/profile`, `/customer/orders`

### Static & Legal Pages
- **About Us:** `/about`
- **Contact Us:** `/contact`
- **FAQs:** `/faqs`
- **Terms of Service:** `/terms`
- **Privacy Policy:** `/privacy-policy`
- **Return Policy:** `/return-policy`
- **Shipment Policy:** `/shipment-policy`
- **Cancellation Policy:** `/cancellation-policy`
- **Data Policy:** `/data-policy`
- **Disclaimer:** `/disclaimer`

## 3. Product Categories
The store primarily deals in the following main categories:
1. **Jewellery:** `/category/jewellery` (Exquisite, premium quality jewelry pieces)
2. **Electronics:** `/category/electronics` (Smart technology, home & personal gadgets)
3. **Watches:** `/category/watches` (Premium analog and digital timepieces)
4. **Glasses:** `/category/glasses` (Stylish sunglasses and eyewear, UV protection)
